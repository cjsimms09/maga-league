Routed from C via Cory: *"K/DEF hold 32 of the top 70 today, 25 with a corrected

One-start positions in this format are **QB, TE, K and DEF** — four of the eight
dedicated starting slots. Composite score, top 70:

| pick | one-start total | QB | TE | K | DEF | RB | WR |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

**32 of 70 at pick 70 reproduces exactly.** It is the ONE-START GROUP, and the
group is entirely QB and TE. **K and DEF are ZERO in the top 70 at every state
measured.** The routed sentence attributes the count to K/DEF; the measurement
belongs to QB/TE.

That matters because the two have opposite remedies. A K/DEF baseline problem is
fixed by baseline depth. A QB/TE problem is the symptom from the mock, and item
24 just measured a mechanism for it from an independent room.

**VORP puts 9 K/DEF in the top 70; the composite puts 0.** So the composite is

correcting a quantity that the shipped recommendation has already discounted.

**And the QB/TE inflation is against the market, not against a slot count.** The
market puts 13 one-start players in the top 70. The composite puts 32. **2.5x.**

## THE STRUCTURAL CLAIM REPLICATES, INDEPENDENTLY

Cory's correction — *rank is relative, you cannot test one position in isolation*
— is right, and it is measurable on our board:

| scenario | K/DEF in top 70 (by VORP) |
|---|---|
| A. shipped depths (QB10 RB21 WR29 TE10 K8 DEF10) | 9 |
| **D. K/DEF corrected ALONE, skill left as shipped** | **9 — no change at all** |
| C. skill deepened by the full flex, K/DEF at slot count | 3 |

**Scenario D is the point, mechanically: correcting K/DEF in isolation moves the
count by exactly zero.** The whole-system correction moves it 9 → 3.

## AND A WARNING INSIDE THE WHOLE-SYSTEM CORRECTION

Scenario C deepens every skill baseline by the FULL flex allocation. It sends
**TE from 8 to 15** of the top 70. The engine's shipped allocation is iterative
and gives the ten flex slots as **WR 9, RB 1, TE 0**.

So "corrected baseline" is not one thing. **The flex allocation is the sensitive
lever**, and a full-flex-to-everyone correction does not close the gap so much as
move it from K/DEF onto TE — the position already over-represented at 18 of 70.
