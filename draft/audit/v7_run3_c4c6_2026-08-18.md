## Why the stores didn't help (the football reading)

The fit tables themselves are sensible: RBs facing 1+ notable arrivals decline
harder than those facing none (median ratio 0.851 vs 0.957 on the 2025 fit);
depth-rank-3+ RBs decline hardest (0.772). The information is REAL — but the
champion already prices it, because v6's market-rank inputs carry the room's
knowledge of every offseason move. After mean-normalization (which removes
level and keeps only within-position shape), what remains is noise the market
had already digested. Same lesson as run two's usage/efficiency null, one
level up: **prior-season stats and offseason context are both priced; the
only run-one signal that survived any gate was source-DISAGREEMENT (WR
FP-vs-own), which is information about the pricers, not the players.**

Declared limitations, not discovered ones: the 2024 C6 fit is one transition
(the QB store starts 2022); C4's `team_change` table never cleared MIN_N for
movers (fewer than 8 qualifying moved-RBs per fit set — the bucket
contributed nothing, stated in `c4_bucket_ns`).
