## 1. PREREGISTRATION — STUDY 1: what actually decides coin-flip picks

**The question (Cory, verbatim): "Is it in the 50/50 picks?"** Operational
form: across every near-tie the machinery can produce from 2023–25, which
pick-time-knowable features actually predicted the eventual winner — with n
and CIs per feature, and "predicted nothing" said per feature where true.

### 1.1 Pair sources (fixed before mining)

**Source A — replay near-ties** (the machinery's own toss-ups). For each
replay season Y ∈ {2023, 2024, 2025}: run the committed replay harness's
PRIMARY configuration (import `draft/tools/draft_replay_2025.py`: onesie
caps QB2/RB7/WR7/TE2, unfiltered walk-forward board, market arm removed) and
reconstruct the exact board state at each of Cory's live skill pick moments
from the replay's own pick log. At each moment, enumerate the legal
candidates exactly as `replay_draft` does (availability, caps, forced-rail)
and collect every pair (chosen, alternative) with
**VORP(chosen) − VORP(alternative) ≤ 5.0 season points** — the near-tie
band, fixed a priori at ~0.3 pts/week (the same order as the engine's own
composite tie constants, TIE_THRESHOLD 2.0 / CLOSE_GAP 3.5, on a
season-points scale). Sensitivity bands 2.0 and 10.0 are reported beside the
primary, never headline. A pair is counted once per season (first pick
moment at which it appears; unordered dedupe). Parity pin: at every
reconstructed moment the enumeration's top candidate must equal the logged
replay pick, or the miner refuses to emit.

**Source B — the league's actual near-ties** (the room's own toss-ups). For
each season Y ∈ {2023, 2024, 2025}: the real main draft from
`league_history.json`; skill picks only (QB/RB/WR/TE), keepers excluded.
Two players form a pair when they are **the same position and drafted
within ≤ 5 overall picks of each other** (each pick paired with the next
same-position pick at gap ≤ 5) — the room's revealed statement that it
priced them within half a round. **No historical ADP archive exists for
these drafts** — the in-draft pick distance is the honest revealed-pricing
substitute, and this limitation is named wherever Source B is quoted.

### 1.2 Outcome (fixed before mining)

- **Primary: realized season-Y points delta.** Realized points = sum of the
  committed weekly stores' scored points, weeks 1–17. A player with no
  scored rows realized 0 points — that is a realized outcome (he returned
  nothing), not missing data; the count of such players is reported.
  **Winner = higher realized total.** Exact ties dropped and counted.
- **Secondary: weeks-won delta** — strict weekly comparisons over the
  CO-ACTIVE weeks (both players have scored rows); pairs with no co-active
  weeks or an equal count are excluded from this arm and counted.

### 1.3 Features (all fixed before mining; per-feature absent ⇒ pair
excluded from that feature's cell and counted — absent is not zero)

Each feature grades as: among pairs where both values are known and
unequal, how often did the feature-favored player win? Direction hypotheses
fixed now; grading is two-sided regardless.

| # | feature | construction (strictly pick-time-knowable) | favored = |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | age | board age back-projected arithmetically (own_model_v2 `board_ages`, the replay's own declared construction) | younger |
| F2 | experience | count of prior seasons (2021..Y−1) with any store row; **censored at 2021 (stores start there) — said wherever quoted** | fewer seasons |
| F3 | prior-season games missed | 17 − (scored-row count in Y−1); row-presence = "was on a field", the stores' rule | fewer missed |
| F4 | TD-share of prior points | (pass_td+rush_td+rec_td points under the frozen table) ÷ Y−1 realized points, from the component stores | LOWER share (TD-regression hypothesis) |
| F5 | team change that offseason | modal Y−1 store team ≠ first-row Y store team. **Named leakage nuance:** the Y team identity was public pre-draft (free agency), but its in-store availability conditions on having played in Y — pairs where either player has no Y row are absent for F5 and counted | stayed put |
| F6 | prior-season ppg | Y−1 realized points ÷ games | higher ppg |
| F7 | late-season trajectory | Y−1 late-window rate (v2's LATE_FROM..17, ≥ LATE_MIN_GAMES) − Y−1 ppg | hotter finish |
| F8 | weekly cv (boom/bust) | sd/mean of Y−1 weekly scored points, ≥ 6 games and mean > 0, else absent | HIGHER cv (weekly-high-league hypothesis; feeds Study 2) |
| F9 | depth-chart proxy | was the player his modal Y−1 team's №1 at his position by realized points; decides only when exactly one of the pair was №1. **PROXY — no historical depth charts exist; said wherever quoted** | the №1 |

**Features absent by design, named in the deliverable table rather than
silently dropped:** ADP velocity (no historical ADP archive for these
drafts); playoff-weeks slate (`playoff_sos_2026.json` is 2025-defenses-for-
2026 only — not period-correct for 2023–25 and therefore NOT graded);
live `depth_chart_order` (2026 board only).

### 1.4 Grading and the decision rule (fixed before mining)

Per feature × per source × pooled: n pairs decided, wins for the favored
direction, p̂, **Wilson 95% CI**. Robustness slice: same-position-only
subset of Source A (Source B is same-position by construction).
**Verdict per feature: PREDICTIVE iff the pooled Wilson 95% CI excludes
0.50 AND pooled n ≥ 30; otherwise the printed verdict is "predicted
nothing (n=…)".** Secondary outcome (weeks-won) is reported beside the
primary and cannot alone make a feature PREDICTIVE.

**Prepared-diff clause (gated, not applied):** iff ≥ 1 feature is
PREDICTIVE, prepare (never apply) a diff reordering
`public/js/draft/verdict.js tiebreakFacts`'s printed facts to the measured
ranking (features ordered by |p̂ − 0.5| among PREDICTIVE ones, the rest in
their current order), and queue it in DECISIONS-NEEDED.md. If nothing
clears, the null is the deliverable ("50/50s are true coin flips — stop
sweating them") and NO diff is prepared — manufacturing a decision from a
null is the exact failure the house rules name.

### 1.5 Leakage discipline

Features for a season-Y pair may open only < Y stores, plus exactly two
named exceptions: the time-invariant board-age arithmetic (the replay's own
declared construction) and F5's read of the Y store's team field (public
pre-draft fact, named above). A tracer records every store open on the
feature path; the test fails on any other ≥ Y read and asserts the tracer
is non-vacuous. Outcome grading (season-Y points) is post-hoc by
definition and runs outside the traced feature path.

## 2. PREREGISTRATION — STUDY 2: variance-portfolio construction

**The question (Cory, verbatim): "Is it in the actual roster
construction?"** — specifically the league-rules edge nobody drafts for:
this league pays **$100 × 15 weekly highs (37.5% of the $4,000 pot)**, and
two same-mean rosters can differ in weekly sd. Does drafting for
roster-level VARIANCE buy enough weekly-high dollars to beat its cost in
H2H record? Both tails priced.

### 2.1 The measuring arm (`draft/tools/variance_portfolio.py`)

Per-player weekly cv from the committed component stores, scored under the
frozen table (the same basis as conditional_value.py): for each player and
each season ∈ {2024, 2025}, weekly mean / sd / cv over scored rows, weeks
1–17, **measurable iff ≥ 6 rows and mean > 0, else absent — never zero**.
Primary per-player cv = the most recent measurable season (2025, else
2024, else absent). Class fallback for absent players (rookies etc.):
per-position unweighted mean cv over players with a measurable 2025 cv who
finished top-40 at the position by 2025 realized points (the
draft-relevant class), with n stated. Artifact:
`draft/data/variance_inputs_2026.json` (`_territory` first). Stack
correlations are NOT remeasured — the committed
`draft/data/conditional_value_2026.json` class table (QB-WR1 r=0.40,
QB-WR2, QB-TE1, WR1-WR2) is the one source of truth.

### 2.2 The portfolio arms (`draft/tools/variance_portfolio.js`)

Overlay in the exact `archetype_policy.js` pattern (its exported
`candidates` / `legalityOwns` helpers imported, the file never edited);
one draft brain (the shipped engine through the real `live_context.js`,
production weights and flags), the overlay only chooses among
engine-endorsed candidates:

- **`shipped`** — engine recs[0] (control).
- **`var_tilt`** — among candidates within **TIE_THRESHOLD (2.0 composite
  points, the engine's own tie constant) of recs[0].score** (skill
  positions only; forced picks and legality warnings never overridden),
  take the candidate with the highest **marginal roster weekly variance**:
  own weekly var + 2·ρ_class·wsd(cand)·wsd(rostered same-NFL-team
  teammate) summed over measured class pairs (QB↔WR, QB↔TE, WR↔WR —
  ρ from the committed conditional_value class table; pairs with no
  measured class, e.g. RB↔anything, contribute nothing and are counted —
  absent, not zero). wsd(p) = cv(p) × proj_mean(p)/16; absent cv → the
  position-class fallback, counted per room.
- **`var_avoid`** — same band, LOWEST marginal variance (the symmetric
  control: if the instrument is fair, the anti-tilt must lose weekly-high
  dollars).

Ties inside the band break to engine order. All three arm parameters are
fixed in this commit, before any ranking room runs.

### 2.3 Rooms and season scoring with the pot priced

Rooms exactly as the archetype study's primary: Cory's real seat and
keepers, the real designated opponent-keeper slate, measured opponent
model (survival.js position probabilities + room-mixture softmax, same
rails), **120 paired seeds (1–120), 3 arms**, seeds 9000+ reserved for
mechanics smoke tests and excluded from every ranking. Same room per seed
across arms (paired design).

Season scoring (new, and the part the archetype instrument could not do —
its limitation 3 is exactly what this study builds):

- weekly team MEANS: `archetype_season.weeklyTeamMeans` unmodified
  (bye-aware optimal lineups on proj_mean).
- weekly team SD, derived per team per week from that week's optimal
  starters: Var(w) = Σ starters' (cv·week-mean)² + 2·Σ ρ_class·wsd·wsd
  over same-NFL-team starter pairs in the measured classes. K/DEF
  contribute no measured cv (stores are offense-only) — a shared bias,
  identical across arms, named. **Two sd treatments, both run: RAW
  (derived as-is) and CALIBRATED (all teams uniformly rescaled so the
  field's mean weekly sd equals the measured league constant 21.3 —
  levels anchored to measurement, relative differences preserved).
  CALIBRATED is primary; an edge that exists in only one treatment is
  not an edge.**
- season MC per room (seeded, 2,000 sims): 15 weeks, every team draws
  Normal(mean_w, sd_team_w), random pairings (no 2026 schedule exists);
  **weekly high pays $100 to the strict weekly max**; standings by (wins,
  points-for); reg-season dollars $250/$125 to 1st/2nd; top-4 playoff,
  bracket 1v4 / 2v3 / final (champodds' pinned format), $675 champ /
  $575 runner-up / semifinal losers by seed $475/$400. Conservation
  identity held by test: Σ over teams of expected total dollars =
  $4,000.00 exactly per room. The 3rd-vs-4th ordering (by seed) is a
  named convention — the league's records don't pin a consolation rule.

### 2.4 Deliverable numbers and the decision rule (fixed now)

Per arm: E[$ weekly-high]/season, E[$ reg], E[$ playoff], E[$ total],
P(weekly high)/wk, P(weekly LOW)/wk (the bust tail, priced), exp_wins,
playoff_prob, bottom3_prob, champ_prob, mean weekly points (the mean cost
of tilting — the "same-mean" premise is CHECKED, not assumed), roster cv
and stacked-pair counts (the mechanism readout). Paired deltas vs shipped
with 95% CIs, plus 3 × 40-seed batch stability.

**The honest question, graded exactly as asked: does var_tilt's
Δ E[$ total] beat shipped?** Prepared-diff clause (gated, not applied):
iff Δ E[$ total] (var_tilt − shipped) is CI-clear positive in **BOTH** sd
treatments AND sign-stable in all three 40-seed batches, prepare (never
apply) a pot-aware tie-break for the seat-plan surface
(`emit_seat_plan.js` archetype_note pattern) and queue it in
DECISIONS-NEEDED.md. Anything less publishes as the null with both tails
shown. `var_avoid` going CI-clear negative on weekly-high dollars is the
instrument's own sanity check and is reported either way.

### 2.5 What this study cannot say (named now, before the numbers)

1. Everything is conditioned on proj_mean and on cv being stable
   season-to-season; cv is measured on n = 6–17 weeks per player-season
   and is noisy — the class-fallback rate is printed per arm.
2. The opponent model is the measured room used as a generator (the same
   new-use caveat the archetype study named); no ADP-room robustness arm
   is run here (the tilt acts inside a 2-point band — opponent-model
   sensitivity at that scale is bounded by the archetype study's spread).
3. The weekly-high contest is priced among 10 equal-structure teams built
   in the same room; a real week's high depends on realized lineups and
   waivers, none of which exist here.
4. Normal weekly scores (champodds' own model); fat tails would favor
   variance-tilting MORE, so the null direction is conservative for the
   tilt and the positive direction is not inflated by it.
