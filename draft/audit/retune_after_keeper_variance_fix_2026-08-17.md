# THE RE-TUNE, RE-RUN AFTER THE KEEPER-VARIANCE FIX — the conclusion survives a bias that favoured it

**2026-08-17, later the same day.**

## WHY THIS RE-RUN EXISTS

The phase-tuning study was re-run this morning against corrected ceilings and
reported: *aggressive endgame upside loses; ceiling 0.0 best at +65.50
CI [+39.17, +92.67]*. Hours later, sweeping the STUDY code (not the board) for
hardcoded dispersion turned up a live defect in the money proxy underneath it:

```python
my_keepers = [{... "weekly_sd": 8.0} for k in board["kept_players"]]
```

A flat literal, while the pool rows two lines above correctly read the board.
Cory's three keepers actually measure **17.63 / 25.81 / 32.46**, so the proxy
understated the variance of his three highest-variance starters by 2.2x-4.1x,
and team weekly sd by **11.1%** (83.44 against 93.81).

**THE BIAS RAN IN THE DIRECTION OF THE CONCLUSION.** This is a weekly-high-pays
league. Understating variance understates the value of variance — so the proxy
was answering "is upside worth paying for?" while tilted against upside. A
finding of "upside loses", produced by an instrument biased against upside, is
not a finding. It had to be re-run before it could be reported as anything.

The headline moved **$1.17**, deep inside its own interval. Correcting a bias
that should have HELPED the upside arms did not help them. Every non-zero
endgame ceiling weight remains worse than default with a CI excluding zero.

**So Cory's "turn upside up in the late rounds" hypothesis loses again — this
time on an instrument that was corrected in its favour first.** That is the
strongest version of this result so far, and the fourth independent line
agreeing (barbell 10/10 losses, empirical draft value, the morning re-tune,
this).

## WHAT THIS STILL CANNOT SAY, unchanged from the prereg

The dispersion on the board remains `proj_mean x a per-cell constant` — it
varies between bands, not within them. So this measures whether leaning on
CROSS-BAND dispersion differences pays. **It cannot say whether an individual
player is worth taking for his upside**, because no per-player dispersion signal
is wired into the board yet. Snap-share volatility (pulled today) is the first
candidate, and wiring it belongs after the draft.

`risk_w` remains uninterpretable this run: `weekly_sd` is still collinear with
the mean, so reporting a risk optimum here would repeat the error being fixed.
