## WHAT THIS MEANS, STATED AGAINST WHAT CORY HOPED

His hypothesis — *"turn up upside as the draft gets later"* — was refuted by a
study that could not test it. **Re-tested on corrected inputs, it is refuted
harder.** The measured shape is the reverse: **mild ceiling exposure early
(0.5), none at all late (0.0)**, where the shipped auto function ramps 0.45 →
0.60 → 0.80 and then holds 0.5 in the endgame.

The intuition behind the hypothesis is sound and common — late picks are cheap,
so swing. The measurement keeps saying that in *this* league, with *these*
inputs, the cheap late pick is better spent on the highest expected points than
on the widest distribution. That is now the **third independent** result
agreeing: the barbell pass (ten arms, ten losses), the empirical draft-value
study, and this.
