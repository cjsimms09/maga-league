## The two apparent disagreements — both mine

**1. `adjusted_adp` disagreed on 10 of 11.** My reimplementation built its player
index from `d['players']`, which **excludes kept players** — they live in
`kept_players`. So `kept_ranks` came back empty, the keeper shift was zero, and
every value was high by exactly `0.3 × (keepers ahead)`. The offsets were 0.9,
0.6 and 0.3 — three, two and one keeper ahead. Fixed by using the full universe;
11/11 then matched exactly. **The engine was right and my check was wrong**,
which is the case independent arithmetic exists to distinguish and the reason
"the numbers agree" is not the same claim as "I recomputed them."

**2. `opportunity_adj` could not be re-derived from `opportunity_z` for two
players.** Not a defect either, but a real observation worth keeping.
`opportunity_z` is stored rounded to 2 dp while `opportunity_adj` carries 4.
Skattebo's stored `adj = +0.0055` implies `z = +0.07333`, stored as `0.07`;
Burden's `adj = −0.0019` implies `z = −0.02533`, stored as `−0.03`. Both are
consistent once solved backwards. `proj_mean` itself matched exactly for all 11.

**FINDING, MINOR AND UNFIXED:** `opportunity_z` is stored as an EXPLANATION of
`opportunity_adj` and does not carry enough precision to reproduce it. Anyone
auditing the adjustment from the field that exists to justify it will find a
mismatch and have to solve backwards, as I did. Not wrong — under-precise. Left
alone deliberately: changing a stored field's precision to serve an audit is a
board change, and the board is nine days from a draft.

### Three harness faults on the way to that result

Each looked like an engine defect. None was.

1. **NaN on 8 of 11.** My ctx passed `targetPick`; the engine reads `nextPick`.
2. **`vona` equal to raw `proj_mean`.** Same cause — with no `nextPick`, VONA
   degenerates to the unadjusted projection instead of a survival-weighted
   marginal.
3. **All 11 deltas non-zero.** I passed no `ctx.weights`, so `scorePlayer` fell
   back to `DEFAULT_WEIGHTS` (`tier 1, need 1, risk 1, ceiling 0.65, bye 1,
   stack 1`) — everything ON. The app initialises `state.weights` to
   `MEASURED_WEIGHTS` at `app.js:52`, which I had not checked before asserting
   what ships.

Fault 3 is worth keeping: I was about to report "the measured weights do not
reach the scorer," which would have been a serious and entirely false finding.
The check that establishes what ships is `app.js:52`, not the constant's own
comment calling itself *"what the tool loads on."*
