## Caveats

* Projections are the board's own — the instrument I have spent the day finding
  faults in. Fair for comparing two players on one board; weak as absolutes.
* Rosters are as drafted. Any waiver move since makes this stale.
* Nothing here models schedule, injury or your read on these players. It is a
  shape argument: **you are long exactly what six teams are short, and short
  exactly what they are long.**
