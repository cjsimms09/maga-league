# Playoff-weeks matchup softness — 2026 (weeks 15-17)

_Generated from `draft/data/playoff_sos_2026.json` by `draft/tools/playoff_sos.py` (2026-08-16). Cory's directive: the title is decided in fantasy playoff weeks 15-17, but the board prices all 17 weeks equally — this is the playoff-slate fact for every skill player, readable without a UI._

**What the number is.** For each team: its NFL week-15/16/17 opponents (2026 schedule, nflverse), scored by how many fantasy points per game each of those defenses allowed to the position **last season (2025), under our league's own scoring**. Softness rank 1 = softest slate of the 32 teams (most points allowed). Raw per-game numbers are in the artifact.

**⚠ How to use it — and how not to.** TILT-BREAKER ONLY. It changes no board number (no projection, no VORP, no rank, no scoring CFG). Last season's defenses shift with personnel and scheme — points-allowed is among the least stable team stats year over year; December weather, home/away and resting starters are not modeled. Between two picks the board already calls even, take the softer January slate. Never reach past a tier for it.

_Tables below cover board top-150 players (a 150-pick draft; deeper names decide nothing) — full 551-player detail is in the artifact. Rank shown is the TEAM's slate rank at that position, so teammates share it. A player never appears in both tables: where a position has fewer than 30 draftable names (QB, TE) the toughest table is honestly shorter rather than recycling the middle._
