But it's cheap (all data already harvested/archived) and high-joy; slot it into gaps starting Aug 23. Voice matters: match the site's existing tone ("Winners win. Losers pick first. That's the deal."). This page should be FUN — a thing people scroll at Thanksgiving, not a spreadsheet.

## 1. Season chapters — one clickable page per year (2023 → present)

Each season page contains:
- **The written recap** (3–6 paragraphs, generated by you at build time from the data, committed as content — no runtime LLM calls): a TRUE HISTORY in **full league-banter voice** — who won, who choked, who got robbed, who lucked into money they didn't deserve, the benchings, the 70-point weeks, the trash-talk-worthy everything. **Crude is correct, cussing is allowed, and EVERYONE gets ripped** — winners for their luck, losers for their choices, the unlucky for their cursed existence, and **Cory most of all** when the data earns it (the 1.06 miss, the 297 bench points, the 8.2 tiebreak — light him up). Name names. The recap voice is governed by the four surviving rules below (§5).
- **Final standings** with record, PF, PA, efficiency%, and the money table (base + weekly highs = total $) — the REAL standings nobody knew until now
- **The weekly-high ledger**: all 15 weeks, winner, score, margin over second (call out sub-2-point races)
- **Playoff bracket** rendered with scores
- **Draft recap**: first two rounds pick-by-pick, plus the season-graded verdicts — best pick (value over ADP by outcome), worst pick, best late-round steal, keeper report card (from 2024 on)
- **Season superlatives**: highest single week, lowest single week (with dishonorable mention), biggest blowout, closest game, longest win/loss streaks, best single-player game, bench catastrophe of the year (highest-scoring benched player in a loss)
- **Era notes** where they apply (2023: "the Whiny Little Bitch League year" — the founding, the rebrand story)

## 2. The All-Time Records Book (one page, the crown jewel)

- **Franchise pages per owner** (all 10, zero turnover makes this clean): year-by-year record, all-time W-L, PF, efficiency trend, money ledger, titles/appearances, head-to-head record vs every other owner (the H2H grid), signature wins, signature disasters
- **All-time records**: highest/lowest weekly score ever, biggest blowout, closest margin, longest streaks, most weekly highs (career + single season), most points in a loss (the Bad Beat record — Richard2121's 2025 season is a monument here), best/worst draft pick ever by outcome-vs-ADP
- **The Money Board**: all-time career earnings, ranked, updated yearly — the table that settles every argument
- **The Dynasty tracker**: titles, finals appearances, playoff W-L (mhagen 4-0 gets its shrine; ds7mmet's 0-3 in openers gets its... monument)
- **Bad Beats Hall of Fame**: auto-detected — lost while scoring top-3 that week; missed playoffs on tiebreak (Cory 2024, by 8.2, with 297 bench points — yes, include it, honesty is house style); missed a weekly high by <2 (the 1.06 and the 0.12 belong here); highest score in a losing effort each season
- **Weird & Unique auto-detections**: ties (if any ever), identical scores, both teams under 80, a defense outscoring a QB1, the highest-scoring benched player ever, weeks where last place beat first

## 4. The self-updating machinery
- All content generates from the L2 archives + harvest outputs (matchups, brackets, drafts, transactions, money ledgers) — one build script, deterministic, provenance-stamped
- **The new season chapter is appended by the ANNUAL BUTTON, not a separate trigger** (consolidation directive, 2026-08-08 — see `annual-button.md` §1b/§1c). The single January run generates the recap, recomputes the records book + Money Board, and inducts new bad beats as **one PR in the button's single PR set**. **Hard ordering:** the chapter generates only AFTER that run's grading + data-correction steps complete — the recap must never cite a number the same run later corrects. The page grows a chapter a year forever with one button-press.
- Current-season page exists during the year in "live" mode (standings + high ledger updating weekly via the existing crons), converting to its final chapter at season end
- Data corrections flow through: if a harvest fix changes a number, the rebuild regenerates affected pages (content is derived, never hand-maintained)

## 5. Guardrails
- Public-page discipline: NOTHING from the war room, dossier behavioral analysis, opponent models, or strategy tooling leaks here — this page shows what happened, never what the machine thinks about anyone's tendencies. The line: results and records are league property; analysis is mine.
- **Recap voice — the four surviving rules (govern the full-banter recap above):**
  1. **Every roast is traceable to a stat — the data licenses the joke.** No stat, no shot. A benching, a tiebreak, a 70-point week, a weekly-high miss by 1.06 — the number is the setup and the punchline. A roast with no stat behind it gets cut at build time.
  2. **Rip outcomes, decisions, and fantasy incompetence ONLY** — never anyone's actual life, family, job, health, or anything real-world sensitive, and **no slurs, ever.** The target is always the fantasy team, the lineup call, the draft reach, the luck. This is a hard content line the generator enforces, not a matter of taste.
  3. **Equal-opportunity — the algorithm counts roasts per owner and nobody escapes a season clean.** A per-owner roast counter runs at build; if anyone (Cory included) has zero shots taken in a season, the generator owes them one before the chapter ships. No untouched owners.
  4. **The champion gets ONE paragraph of genuine respect** — earned, specific, stat-backed — before we get back to disrespecting everyone else.
- Every generated recap ends with the season's money table, because that's the scoreboard this league actually plays for (they just don't all know it yet).
